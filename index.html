How the six weeks work
  1. Start with the listed weight, sets, and reps.
  2. If every prescribed set and rep is completed cleanly, add 5 lb to upper-body barbell lifts next time.
  3. For lower-body barbell lifts, add 10 lb after a complete clean session.
  4. If reps are missed or form breaks down, repeat the same weight.
  5. Week 4 is a lighter deload with fewer sets.
  6. Weeks 5–6 rebuild and finish from your last successful working weights.

Fat Bell maximum: 62 lb. Kettlebell maximum: 40 lb. Log the weight of one implement; for unilateral work, log reps per side after completing both sides.

The seventh session is deliberately easy. Choose recovery-only when fatigue calls for it. Continue missed sessions in order; do not double up to catch up.

Stop a movement for sharp pain or returning weakness. Reduce, change, or skip movements that worsen your back. This program is not a rehabilitation prescription.
